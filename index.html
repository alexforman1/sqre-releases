Download Sqre for Mac

Sqre.dmg · about 300 MB · Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later

On a phone? Open this page on your Mac to download Sqre.

Three steps

  1. Open the download

    When it finishes, open Sqre.dmg: click it in your browser’s downloads, or find it in your Downloads folder.

  2. Drag Sqre to Applications

    In the window that opens, drag the Sqre icon onto the Applications folder next to it.

  3. Open Sqre

    Open Sqre from Applications. The first time, your Mac says Apple couldn’t check Sqre for malware (this test build hasn’t been through Apple’s check): close that message. Then open System Settings, click Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, click Open Anyway next to Sqre, and confirm with your password. A short setup takes it from there.

Accounts

You’ll need a TypeSafe account for Sqre’s AI (and optionally OpenRouter). Setup walks you through it.

What you need

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 Ventura or later.

Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac: “Chip” says Apple M-something.

Sqre keeps itself up to date. You only download it once.
